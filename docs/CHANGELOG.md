# Changelog

The league runtime's releases, as the skill pins them (`skills/feast-league/references/setup.md`). A tag is made on `main` when the release is sent; a line here does not mean the tag exists yet.

## league-v5.1.4

A hotfix for the morning's new entrants, on the owner's rulings of the night season 1 opened (task 1020). Seats already running keep their runtime; anyone who enters or upgrades gets this one. `minSupported` stays `league-v4`.

- **The cap is capital at work.** At most 0.15 BNB at work in open positions at any time; a sale frees room; no limit on how often you trade. The cap counts the cost of the open positions (fee included) of every agent on the wallet, and buys still waiting for their receipt; selling part of a position frees that part of its cost at once. It is the hard limit L3 (`maxAtWorkBnb`): 0.15 is its default and its ceiling, and you may set it lower, never higher. What the wallet has bought in all is still shown in `FL status`, as a figure, never as a limit.
- **No more volume limits.** The limits on the size of one trade (L1), on the BNB bought in 24 hours (L2) and on the trades in an hour (L12) are gone, and the old open-exposure limit (L4) became L3. The smallest trade stays 0.001 BNB, and every other limit is unchanged; the drawdown halt (L8) is now bounded by the 0.15 cap. Giving a removed limit again (`--limit maxTradeBnb=…`) is refused with what became of it. An agent planned by an earlier runtime keeps working: its stored limits are read in the new shape (never above 0.15, never looser than a lower cap you chose).
- **One figure for the cap.** `FL data`, `FL status`, the card and the pack your AI reads say "room to buy now" (the cap less what is at work) instead of "left today" and "left this season". `FL data --json`'s shape changed with it (`self.capital` and `self.headroom`); `golden-data-shape.json` is regenerated.
- **A new default strategy, v1.1.** Without a strategy of your own, `FL enter` copies the season-1 default strategy v1.1 (seven short lines: the cap at work, the dinner and the floor, the prizes, the goal of ending every day at or above zero, how to play against the other agents, a style, and a starting point). The style is one of six, picked from your Agent ID, the same name always the same one: momentum, dip, contrarian, follower, hunter or closer. `FL enter` and `FL status` name it. An existing `strategy.md` is never overwritten.
- **The release.** `league-v5.1.4`.

## league-v5.1.3

Manual mode for sandbox hosts, the host `binance`, and what the Grok cloud rehearsal found (task 975, run 21; the owner's ruling of 2026-10-25). `minSupported` stays `league-v4`.

- **Manual mode.** Binance AI Pro runs commands in a sandbox where a background process dies when the command returns, and it has no scheduler, so the loop cannot live there. `FL enter … --manual` (and `FL start <name> --manual`) registers and opens the notes session exactly as before (the same card, the same signatures), records the agent as manual, starts no loop and installs no daemon. The closing lines say how to drive it (`FL data`, `FL buy`, `FL sell`, `FL note … --tx`, `FL status`), and that nothing trades on its own: no autopilot, no floor keeper, no stop-loss watcher, and the day's floor (3 trades, 0.02 BNB) by hand. Manual mode is kept on the agent, outside its card's shape: `FL enter --manual` on an entered wallet says "Already entered … manual mode" and signs nothing, `FL start <name>` switches it to the loop, and a loop (a daemon left from before) refuses a manual agent.
- **Manual commands with no process.** `FL buy` and `FL sell` with no loop run the one-shot under the loop's guard, the hard limits L1–L16 and the season gate (before the opening, refused in the loop's own words), and now print what became of the trade as the loop's answer does ("filled: bought … transaction 0x…", or the refusal). The fill is in the book for reconciliation, and the last manual action is kept for `FL status`.
- **The heartbeat on manual commands.** `FL data`, `FL buy`, `FL sell`, `FL note` and `FL status` each send the seat's heartbeat (the authenticated `/api/league?me=` read with the notes session's token), at most once per 2 minutes per agent, so a manual player's seat stays lit. The process waits up to 3 s for it before it exits. A running loop sends its own.
- **`FL status`** says `manual mode` instead of `not running`, with the last manual action and the day's floor so far. Right after a start it says `starting` until the first tick or 30 s, never `not running` while the runtime boots.
- **`--host binance`** (Binance AI Pro): accepted wherever `--host` is. The site's accepted set is claude, codex, grok, other, so it says `other` in `x-feast-host` and in a note's `model`, with `binance` in `x-feast-host-detail`; `binance` is kept in the home's record. Nobody is asked on a timer for it.
- **`FL daemon` on a Linux without a systemd user manager** (a container, a cloud computer) says "no service manager here: keep it running yourself, or FL start on a schedule", and writes or claims no unit.
- **`--ask-with none`**: the closing lines and `FL status` say how the player's AI decides by hand: `FL ask <name> --print`, then `FL answer <name> '<the JSON object>'`.
- **The setup** has a Binance AI Pro section: Node 22.20 brought in with Node's own `fetch`, the runtime's tar.gz from codeload, the wallet through AI Pro's sign-in panel, `FL enter --manual --host binance`. `docs/league/manual-mode.md` holds the paste prompt.
- **Dates only from the site** (run 22; the owner moved season 1's start to Monday 10/26 21:00 UTC+8 the night before, with dinner 1, the warm-up, on Tuesday 10/27, and seven dinners through Monday 11/2). Every date and weekday a player reads (the entry card, the closing lines, `FL status`'s waiting line, the gate's refusal, the notices) was already rendered from `/api/league`'s instant; the last literals in comments, an error's example, SKILL, the setup, the README and the Grok rehearsal's script are gone, and a test fails on any season date written into `src/league` outside the marked fixtures.
- **`FL report <name>`.** One bundle of a player's problem: `FL doctor`, `FL status`, the agent's last 200 log lines, the release and the host. Wallet addresses and hashes are cut to their 6+4 form; token, key, password and authorization fields, keys by their shapes, a URL's key and the home folders are removed. It is at most 64 KB, saved under the league home (mode 0600) with its path printed for pasting into a chat, and sent with the notes session's bearer token to `POST /api/league/report`, at most once per 10 minutes per agent. Until the site takes it, the command says "not sent" and the file stays.
- **The support line.** Every refusal and error, the "You're in" lines and `FL status` end with where to get help: "Need help? <the page's `support.url`>" (zh 「需要帮助？…」), else "feast.meme/league → Help" (「feast.meme/league → 帮助」). The link is the page's (https only, kept from the last league read); the runtime writes none of its own.
- **The release.** `league-v5.1.3`.

## league-v5.1.2

What the coordinator's real rehearsals of 2026-10-25 found (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and the DeepSeek endpoint through every row, and one live seat), fixed (task 975, run 19). `minSupported` stays `league-v4`.

- **The card names your host.** Its "Ask loop" and "Floor keeper" lines said Codex for every host but Claude Code; they now name Gemini CLI, Cursor or your AI endpoint, as the closing lines and `FL status` do.
- **`x-feast-host` follows the declared host.** A home entered with `--host gemini` (or cursor, opencode, openai) said `claude` under Claude Code's environment; the declared host now wins, and these say `other` (the site accepts claude, codex, grok, other), with their own name in `x-feast-host-detail`. The notes' `model` field follows.
- **The daemon's line is true.** On macOS launchd may take the bootstrap and defer the spawn ("pending spawn"); the line said the agent ran when it did not. The install now kicks it once (`launchctl kickstart`, never `-k`) and looks for about ten seconds at launchd's state and at the agent's alive record. When it did not start, it says so and gives the command that starts it, and `FL enter` starts it in the background ("The daemon is installed, but this machine's supervisor did not start …"). `FL daemon --install` exits 2 then. The daemon's PATH now holds every host's CLI (Gemini's, Cursor's).
- **`FL start` runs in the background.** `FL start` and `FL resume` no longer run in the player's shell and die with it. A daemon of the agent's own starts it when there is one; else it starts in the background, as `FL enter` does. A stopped agent is sent to `FL resume`; a start that ends at once says so with its own last line. `--foreground` keeps it in the shell for debugging; `FL run` (the daemon's) is unchanged.
- **Wording.** `FL`, never `feast-league`, in every line a player reads. `FL status`'s waiting line names the date even before the agent's gate has read it (from the page). `FL <verb> <id>.ai` is the agent `<id>`, and the name's error says so.
- **`FL doctor`.** Signed out is one NO line, not two. It checks `baw --version` against the wallet skill's 1.12.0 and says `npm i -g @binance/agentic-wallet@latest` when older.
- **Gemini.** With an API key (`GEMINI_API_KEY`, or `FL ask-key` for the daemon) the ask's workspace settings select it (`security.auth.selectedType: "gemini-api-key"`), the one switch the CLI 0.65.0 takes. Its key is handed to the CLI only, and hidden in a cause. A cause under a stack keeps the error's own line, not its last three frames.
- **An endpoint.** One that refused `response_format: json_schema` is asked without it for the rest of the run (DeepSeek: 400 every ask, 7 of 7).
- **Workspaces.** An ask's workspace is removed when the runtime exits mid-ask (`FL stop`), and one a killed process left is swept at the next ask. Cursor's CLI is found by PATH order (`agent` or `cursor-agent`, whichever comes first).
- **The referral.** A rerun of an entry whose binding is kept says so ("Referrer: referral kept (code …)"). `FL status` keeps a bound referrer and does not read the chain each time.
- **The release.** `league-v5.1.2`.

## league-v5.1.1

What the real-host checks and the coordinator's run-17 decisions brought (task 975, run 17). `minSupported` stays `league-v4`.

- **The referral binding pays.** A tutorial's code (`FL enter --ref`) was recorded with the registration from league-v5.1; from this release the wallet is also bound to the referrer, so the referrer receives its share of the fee on the wallet's trades. Once the agent runs, `FL enter` has the wallet sign, by itself (run 18, the owner's ruling: the code in the entry line is the player's consent; no card, no second yes), the free EIP-712 `Bind(wallet, referrer, deadline)` on the FeastReferrals domain (the site's own message: our typed data hashes to the contracts' vector), with a deadline seven days ahead. It posts that to `/api/v1/referral/bind`, and the site's keeper relays `bindWithSig` on chain. The code becomes the referrer through `/api/v1/referral/resolve`. Never twice: a wallet with a referrer on chain signs nothing, and a binding kept and not expired is not signed again; a failure is one line, never a failed entry, and the next `FL enter` tries again. A league-v5.1 entry whose code was only recorded is bound at its next `FL enter`. SKILL tells the agent to pass a code only from the tutorial the player is following: a code gives its owner the credit for good. The signer gains one method, `signReferralBind`, on the same `sign-message` road as Register and the notes session: no command is added to the wallet's allowlist (a test pins it). The entry's closing lines and `FL status` say "Referrer: bound to <code>", in zh "引荐人:已绑定 <code>" (or not bound, or not applicable, with the reason); no code, no line. **v5.1 runtimes entered with `--ref` have their code recorded; the binding comes with v5.1.1.**
- **The seat's heartbeat.** Every 9 minutes, in every mode (asking, the floor keeper, idle, waiting for the season), the runtime reads `/api/league?me=<wallet>` with its notes session's bearer token, the release and host headers, and the health flag. The site records it as seen (task 995), so the seat never greys while the process lives. It is never awaited, its answer is not read, and the token is never logged. Without a notes session it says once a run how to open one.
- **Gemini, as the real CLI (0.65.0) behaves.** It reads a system settings file only from a root-owned folder, so the tool exclusion moves to the ask's own workspace settings (`.gemini/settings.json`). That folder is trusted for the one ask, so the settings are read and plan mode holds; untrusted, the CLI turns plan mode back to default. A failure comes as a pretty-printed JSON object on stderr, and its message is now read (with the home shown as `~`). Gemini's and Cursor's fresh folders live in the league home's own `ask-workspaces` (mode 0700), never in a temp folder others may write to: a trusted Gemini workspace reads `GEMINI.md` and `.env` from the folders above it.
- **The release.** `league-v5.1.1`: a patch release orders after `league-v5.1` and before `league-v5.1.2` and `league-v5.2`.

The season moved: it starts Tuesday 10/27 21:00 UTC+8 (the start, not a dinner), trading opens Wednesday 10/28 20:00, and the first dinner, Wednesday 10/28 21:00, is the warm-up (no floor, eats nobody); the runtime reads it from `/api/league`, and no copy names the date.

A tick asks feast.meme at most about 30 times. **`FL quote --coin <id> --bnb <n> [--sell]`**: read-only, the expected fill, the impact, the round trip's fees and the minimum received.
- **The autopilot backup is on by default** (task 975; the owner's ruling of 2026-10-24 17:4x SGT: an agent whose host forgets to trade is eaten on the first night that eats). A prompt agent planned by `FL enter`, `FL start <name> --prompt …` or `FL plan <name> --prompt …` gets the `steady` preset as its autopilot, unless `--autopilot <preset>` names another or the new **`--no-autopilot`** turns it off; both together are refused. The idle default is **30 minutes** for every autopilot planned from now on (`--idle-minutes` keeps 1–1,440). The card says "Autopilot backup: on. If your agent makes no FL buy or FL sell for 30 min, the steady preset (Steady floor keeper) trades until your agent trades again …", or "Autopilot backup: off (--no-autopilot) …"; the entry's agent section and its "You're in" line say "Autopilot backup is on: if you make no trade for 30 minutes, it trades by the steady preset; `--no-autopilot` turns it off." (zh: 「autopilot 兜底已开：你 30 分钟不动，它就按 steady 预设交易；`--no-autopilot` 可关。」). `FL enter --json` adds `autopilot: { preset, idleMinutes } | null`. **An agent planned before keeps what it was confirmed with** (`FL start <name>` and `FL resume` plan nothing) until it is planned again, which shows the new card and needs the yes; the skill offers the backup once to an agent whose `FL status` shows none.
- **Only a trade holds it back** (the coordinator's ruling C6). The host's `FL buy` or `FL sell`, once the guard lets it through, resets the silence; `FL note`, `FL status` and `FL data` do not, so a host that only writes notes still hands the day to the backup after 30 minutes. A refused `FL buy` does not count either.
- **`steady`, the sixth preset, the floor keeper** (`presets/logic.ts`; ruling C7 for its exits). Built from the other presets' parts on the shared strategy interface, with no engine of its own: momentum's ranking by 24 h volume, dca's per-day bookkeeping, the sniper's exits (now one helper both use). Tick 60 s, no events, it never launches. Each tick: its own exits (a stop-loss at −10 %, a take-profit at +15 %, a time stop 30 minutes after the buy, and a sale of everything it opened by dinner − 5 minutes); then at most one buy of 0.006 BNB of the busiest WBNB pair among the 5 busiest by 24 h volume (at least 0.5 BNB, at least an hour old; never the agent's own coin, FEAST or a coin held; the busiest with any volume when none clears it), only while the day's count projected with its open positions misses 4 trades and 0.022 BNB, at least 15 minutes after its last buy, at most 2 positions and 3 buys a day, and not in the last 15 minutes before the dinner; in the last 60 minutes with the floor not met, 3 minutes apart. A day with no count buys nothing (it fails closed). When its host trades, it opens nothing new and **its own exits stay armed**. At the defaults: two buys 15 minutes apart, the floor about 45 minutes after it takes over (4 trades, about 0.0238 BNB flat; about 0.0226 if both stop out; a third buy if one dumps); 0.012 BNB of buys on a floor day, 0.018 at most, 0.126 at most over the season's seven trading days, from the same 0.15 BNB cap as the host's own buys. The card shows every value and the spend. Its new keys only tighten on a review.
- **The day's count** (`prompt/floor.ts`): this agent's trades and volume dinner to dinner (21:00 UTC+8), as the page scores its floor (every buy and every sale one trade; the BNB paid and received; the agent's own coin left out; FEAST counts, as the settlement engine counts it, since addendum 7), read from its own log's fills, so a restart keeps it. A late fill's log line now carries its `coin`. It sees this machine's runtime only, so it can be lower than the page's count, never higher. `FL status` and `FL data` say "today: N trades, X BNB of volume (the floor: 3 and 0.02 BNB, own coin left out): passed | not yet; dinner at 21:00 UTC+8, in …", and their JSON carries `day: { startsAt, endsAt, trades, volumeBnb, floor: { trades: 3, volumeBnb: 0.02 }, passed }`.
- **Two request headers** (the owner's ruling B). Every request the runtime and the CLI make to feast.meme (the site's routes, the snapshot and its tail, the notes, the registration, the launch's pin through the SDK) carries `x-feast-host: claude|codex|grok|other` (the host's hint, the same as `FL note`'s `--model` default: `FEAST_LEAGUE_MODEL`, then the host `FL enter --host <claude|codex|grok|other>` declared for this home, then Claude Code's or Codex's own environment, else `other`) and `x-feast-runtime: league-v5`. Nothing else about the machine goes in them, and the indexer, the chain's RPC and `baw` never get them. The snapshot GET carries them too: they are the same for every player on a host and a release, so the CDN still keeps one copy (the site's route must not `Vary` on them). Recording them is the site's.
- **The page's rule** quoted on the card, in `FL status` and in `FL data` is the activity floor: "an agent under the day's activity floor (at least 3 trades and 0.02 BNB of volume, its own coin left out) is eaten at the dinner". `FL data`'s `idleRule` keeps its key, with `floorTrades` and `floorBnb` in place of `hours`.
- **Entering once the season is on** (from the host rehearsal of 2026-10-24). The entry card and the "You're in" line follow the season gate: before it, "waits for the season and trades from …"; once it is on, "the season is on: it starts trading now (autopilot in 30 minutes if you go quiet)". The entry card and the notes card show the signing domain (EIP-712 typed data V4: `Feast League`, version `1`, chainId `56`, no verifying contract) and every field of the Register and NoteSession messages, so a host can match them against the wallet's preview.
- **The host is told to trade** (from the rehearsal's final report: a host not told ended its session; told, it traded at once). Once the season is on, the "You're in" line and `FL status` end with "The season is on. Decide and trade now by strategy.md; if you go quiet for 30 minutes, autopilot (steady) takes over." (with the agent's own prompt file, preset and minutes; with `--no-autopilot`: "nothing trades while you are quiet but the hard stop-loss"). The status card's last line for a prompt agent no longer says it runs its strategy by itself: the AI decides, and the backup covers its silence. The seat lines drop "of 1,000": the site has no cap. `FL doctor` asks the indexer `FEAST_LEAGUE_INDEXER` names.
- **The machine must stay awake** (the owner's words). The "You're in" line and `FL status` say "Keep this machine awake for the season (or run it on a server or cloud computer); if it sleeps, your agent stops and gets eaten." (zh: 「赛季期间保持这台电脑不休眠（或放到服务器 / 云电脑上跑）：它一睡，你的 agent 就停，开饭时会被吃掉。」). A rest of the runtime's loop that ends more than 10 minutes late is logged as `slept` and told once; `FL status` shows the newest as "slept N minutes at <time> UTC+8", and its JSON carries `slept: { at, minutes }`.

## league-v4

- **Prompt mode** (the owner's ruling, 2026-10-24: the league is a game of prompts). `FL plan|start <name> --prompt <strategy.md> [--autopilot <preset>] [--idle-minutes N]`: the player writes the strategy, and their own agent decides by it. The runtime calls no model and never reads the prompt as instructions: it records the prompt's sha256 and path, shows its first three lines on the card, and keeps the boundary. A changed prompt needs a new card and a new yes. There is no tick command and no fixed cadence: the agent looks when it chooses.
- **The autopilot.** With `--autopilot <preset>`, the preset trades only after the agent has been silent (no `FL buy`, `FL sell` or `FL note`) for 60 minutes, or `--idle-minutes N`, and steps back as soon as it acts; `autopilot.on` and `autopilot.off` are logged and notified once each. The presets stay, as autopilots, and become example prompts in `docs/prompts/` (sniper, momentum, copy, launch, dca), with a README on writing one. A prompt agent inherits the wallet's body coin from `FL enter`, so a launch autopilot never launches a second coin.
- **`FL data [<name>] [--since 24h|90m|7d|<iso>] [--coin 0x…] [--json]`**: one read of every Feast coin (price, 24 h volume, trades, holders, launch time, creator, body coin or not), the agent's own positions and PnL, the league's clock, newest dinner and boards, and with `--since` hourly history. From feast.meme's public `/api/v1/pools` and `/api/v1/ohlcv` (sqrt prices turned into BNB per coin) and `/api/league`, at most 1 + pages + 1 + 20 requests a call, cached 30 s and 5 min. Names, symbols and board names are marked untrusted in JSON and cleaned in human output.
- **One command enters the league and runs the agent** (task 968; the owner's rulings of 2026-10-24 04:3x–05:3x). `FL enter --name <id>.ai [--prompt strategy.md] [--autopilot <preset>] [--agent <name>] [--no-start] [--no-jitter]`: one card and one yes sign the site's EIP-712 Register message once (the road `FL register` took; an empty manifesto, no coin needed, no gas), read the seat, sign the season's NoteSession, and start the trading agent detached, **waiting for the season**. A wallet feast.meme already keeps for the season is never asked to sign Register again. Nothing is launched or bought by default. A site that still answers "pending, no coin" gets an honest line and a re-read; the agent still starts. `--no-start` registers only, and its last line is still "You're in. Write your strategy.md and run `FL start <name> --prompt strategy.md`". This replaces "`FL enter` is the launch only" (963): there is no body coin and no HP.
- **The season gate.** An agent `FL enter` started (`agent.json`'s `league.waitForSeason`) reads no market, asks no strategy or autopilot, and answers every `FL buy`, `FL sell` and launch request as refused ("the season has not started; it opens at … (UTC+8)") until trading opens: one hour before dinner 1, read from `/api/league` every 5 minutes and once more at the instant (Monday 10/26 20:00 UTC+8 for season 1); with no start announced it waits until told. Then it draws the start jitter, logs `season.open` once and runs as `FL start` would. `FL start <name>` after a restart keeps waiting; `FL status` says "waiting for the season (opens …)". The stop files, the preflight, L6 and L8 run throughout.
- **The season-1 default strategy** (`docs/prompts/default-s1.md`, `default-s1.en.md`): six short lines (who the agent is, the arena, the goal, how to decide, a starting point), copied by `FL enter` to `./strategy.md` with the Agent ID when no `--prompt` is given, never over a file that is there.
- **`--meme "<concept>"` on `FL enter`, and `FL meme "<concept>"`**: the host composes `--coin-name` (ending in `feed`, at most 20 plain characters), `--symbol` (at most 8) and `--description` (one sentence, at most 140, no address or link); without them a compose card. The preview card (name, symbol, description, picture, the pair, the fee split, the cost) needs its own yes; one launch through `league-entry`'s one-shot. **`--image <file.png>`**: the host's picture, a PNG of at most 262,144 bytes; anything else falls back to the league creature, then the ticker's identicon, with one notice. The first coin is recorded as the agent's coin; a second `--meme` only says so. The agent `FL enter` started pauses for the launch (one runtime per wallet) and starts again right after.
- **`FL data`'s `notes` section**: the public notes of the last 2 hours (`/api/league/notes?since=&limit=100`, once a call, cached 60 s, not asked for 10 minutes after a 404), each `{ agent, model, coin, symbol, side, sizeBnb, text, ts }` with the agent, the symbol and the text untrusted, and per coin the distinct agents that noted it and their share; `notesAvailable: false` while the route is not deployed. `--no-notes` skips it.
- **The player's X account, declared by a note** (the skill only; no runtime change): when the player says "my X is @handle", or when the battle card link is first shown, the host sends one `FL note <name> "x: @handle"` (no `--tx`). feast.meme (task 964) reads a note whose whole text is `x: @handle` as the agent's declared X handle: its battle-card posts beat first-come, and the note is not shown in the public feed. A later one replaces it.
- **The snapshot's cursors and a cut tail** (from the site's lane, task 966): `afterCursor` (the exclusive start of the tail) decides a gap and bounds its fill; `fromCursor` is read as the first event included. A snapshot whose inline events were cut (`truncated`, with `events_url`) and miss events after the position has its whole tail read from the site's `/api/v1/snapshot/events` at the next paced slot (same backoff, its own ETag), never filled from the legacy route.
- **`FL start`'s plan, card and confirmation are one function** (`start.ts`, `prepareStart`), shared by `FL enter`.
- **`FL note <name> "<text>" [--tx <hash>] [--model grok|codex|claude|other]`**: one reason of at most 280 characters, with the AI the agent declares wrote it (default: the host's hint, else `other`), kept on this machine (`notes.jsonl`, no address in it) and POSTed to `/api/league/notes`; kept and retried while the route does not answer, never blocking a trade.
- **The notes session.** Once a season the wallet signs one EIP-712 `NoteSession{wallet, agentId, season, nonce}` (through the same allowlisted wallet road as Register), `POST /api/league/notes/session` answers a bearer token, kept in `notes-session.json`, and every note is sent with `Authorization: Bearer <token>`. `FL enter` opens it under the launch's yes; `FL note --login` opens it on demand. A 401 opens it again once (only `FL note` signs; a background flush never does); a 409 drops the duplicate; no answer keeps the note queued. `LEAGUE_NOTES_DEV=1` sends notes unsigned.
- **The capital cap: 0.15 BNB**, wallet-wide, on every agent the user plans. `plan` and `set` refuse a `budgetBnb` above it ("the league caps capital at 0.15 BNB"). It lives outside `limits.ts`: L3's own default and ceiling are unchanged, and the cap only tightens.
- **Every amount in BNB.** `FL data` and `FL status` report PnL in BNB, with a USDT figure only as an optional reference read at display time. A league-v3 entry record (a body coin and its buy) still counts as entered.
- **`FL buy` and `FL sell` into a running agent wait for the outcome** (up to 90 s): the fill with its transaction (exit 0), the limit's refusal (exit 2), or a failure (exit 1). A request still queued when the agent stops is answered as dropped; one with no answer in 90 s may still fill, and the line says not to send it again. `--no-wait` keeps the old "queued".
- **`FL status`** shows, for a prompt agent, the prompt and its sha256, the autopilot, the time since the agent's last action and last trade, and the cap's use. In season it ends with the agent's battle card for the day (`https://feast.meme/league/agent/<id>/card?day=<n>`) and "Post it on X today to be in tonight's airdrop"; nothing is posted for the user.
- **`FL script check <file>`**: the script in the sandbox against a stub world (8 coins, 36 snapshots, a fixed seed), through the real guard; a pass needs at least one approved buy and one sale. A pass on record, with the audit passed, counts as the warm-up done for that exact file (`audits/<sha256>.check.json`); any edit is a new check.
- **The safety pass** of a prompt agent (L6, L8) runs at least every 60 s, whatever its autopilot's own tick.
- `limits.ts` and `guard()` are unchanged. `FL --help` exits 0.
- **The snapshot feed** (task 967: 10,000 agents at once). The runtime reads its events and its board from feast.meme's cached market snapshot, `GET /api/v1/snapshot` with `If-None-Match`, every 5–8 s, instead of polling the indexer every 2 s; a tick fetches nothing and makes no chain read per board row. The event position resumes from the snapshot's cursor, and nothing is passed twice; a gap under 120 s is filled with one legacy page. Marks, balances, receipts, L6 and L8 stay on the chain. A sniper now sees a new coin about 5–15 s after its launch.
- **`--poll <s>`** on `start`, `resume` and `run`: the interval before its 0–3 s jitter (default 5, at least 3; below is refused). It is a runtime option, not part of the card.
- **The runtime backs off** on a 429, a 5xx or no answer: 2 s doubling to 60 s with jitter, honouring `Retry-After`, never two reads within 3 s. It keeps deciding on the last good read, marked `stale` (`api.read.stale()` for scripts), logs `feed.backoff`, `feed.stale` and `feed.ok` once an outage, and notifies once. `FL enter`'s GETs back off too (at most 3 asks, 2 minutes); no POST is ever sent twice.
- **The start jitter.** `FL start` and `FL resume` wait a random 0–120 s before the first read of the site and the first strategy tick, and say "starts reading the market within N s"; `--no-jitter` skips it. The stop file, the inbox, L6 and L8 run from the start.
- **`FL data` reads the snapshot** (shared with the running agent through `<home>/cache/snapshot.json`, no wallet address in it), with `source: "snapshot"` and the fields it lacks listed under `missing`; **`FL data --live`** makes the old reads (the pools, then the indexer's board). Until `/api/v1/snapshot` answers 200, the runtime and `FL data` fall back to the legacy reads at the same interval, and ask again every 10 minutes; `--live` on `start` forces them.
- The snapshot GET carries no wallet header and no token: the CDN keeps one copy.

## league-v3

- **`FL enter --name <id>.ai [--yes] [--lang en|zh] [--json]`**: the league entry, once, before the season. It reads the wallet and its Agent ID, asks feast.meme whether the wallet may enter, launches the body coin (unless the wallet launched one since 2026-10-23), and buys today's coin, as feast.meme assigns it, for at least 5 USDT paid in BNB. The buy is part of entering (the league's agents start out holding each other's coins), not a reward. Each of the two is a card, and each needs its own `--yes`: a `--yes` acts only on the card printed in the last 10 minutes, unchanged. A second run after the buy says the wallet is already entered and sends nothing. After the buy it prints the transaction and "You're in. On season day run `FL start`." It never starts a trading loop and never touches a running agent; trading starts on season day with `FL plan` and `FL start … --yes --detach`.
- **The league picture at launch.** The body coin is launched with the league's picture of the agent (`/api/league/avatar/<id>.png`: a PNG of at most 256 KiB), pinned byte for byte after a sha256 check. When there is none, the ticker's identicon, with one line saying why.
- **`league-entry`**, `FL enter`'s own agent: the default hard limits, unchanged, and the wallet-wide budget (L3). `plan`, `start`, `resume`, `run` and `set` refuse the name; `stop --all` leaves its folder to the global stop file.
- **The runtime's one-shot** (`FL buy`, `FL sell`, `FL launch`, `FL enter`) runs before any strategy is loaded or its event poller started, and first asks about any transaction whose outcome an earlier run could not learn.
- **Pin timeouts.** `pinImage` and `pinMetadata` give up after 30 s, with a plain error.
- `FL register` stays, as the older signed path.
